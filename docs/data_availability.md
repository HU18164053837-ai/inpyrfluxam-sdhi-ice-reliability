# Data availability and anonymous-review separation

The curated reproducibility materials are released at https://github.com/HU18164053837-ai/inpyrfluxam-sdhi-ice-reliability.
No repository DOI, article DOI, acceptance or publication status is claimed.
The archive supports recalculation of the saved-point statistical summaries and
regeneration of the existing figures, but does not retrain Web-ICE or reproduce
historical prediction generation from restricted/externally obtained workbooks.
Third-party laboratory reports, publisher full texts/SI and original training
workbooks are excluded; provenance links and factual trial identifiers remain.

This repository identifies the authors and is not an anonymous review attachment.
The original anonymous data/code ZIP remains separate and unchanged. The main
manuscript, SI, title page, cover letter and submission correspondence are not
distributed here or edited by this release. Authors should decide how to disclose
this public link to the editor under the journal's double-anonymous arrangements.

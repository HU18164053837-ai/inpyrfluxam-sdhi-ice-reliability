# Methodological scope

The archive re-evaluates saved Web-ICE version 4.0 predictions, not newly trained
models. Inpyrfluxam and isopyrazam are external validation candidates absent from
the examined public paired-training snapshot; complete trial-level independence
from every historical model-development asset was not established. Six compounds
with known training overlap are conditional whole-compound holdout internal
analyses and must be reported separately. Thirty species–compound endpoint
summaries support 90 dependent directions, not 90 independent experimental units.

The identity-transfer baseline assigns the measured surrogate LC50 directly to
the target. It is a transparent model-gain comparator, not a validated substitute
for a regulatory model. Surrogate selection and directional patterns remain
exploratory. Saved confidence intervals for mean responses are not individual
prediction errors. Target assay intervals do not propagate all surrogate/model
uncertainty; missing assay intervals remain missing. Hazard categories do not
measure exposure-based environmental risk. Structural conservation and limited
redocking results provide context, not causal validation of whole-organism toxicity.

No new model training, docking, dynamics or chemical selection is performed by
the public reproduction workflow. Figures are regenerated solely to check that
existing plotting code runs; supplied frozen figure files remain authoritative.

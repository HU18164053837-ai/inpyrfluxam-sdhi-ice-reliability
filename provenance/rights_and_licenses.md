# Rights and source attribution

This release was screened from the technically validated IEAM v1.0.2 archive.
It is a curated research compilation, not a redistribution of original study
reports or the Web-ICE training database. No new blanket open licence is assigned.
The historical rights CSV is retained unchanged; its statements that no public
repository existed describe the frozen editorial stage, not the current release.

The trial-evidence CSVs were reviewed as curated facts and identifiers rather
than complete or reconstructable individual-organism records. They contain no
individual mortality observations, full concentration-response series, raw
laboratory spreadsheets or full original study reports. Repeated summaries are
not additional experiments. An isolated numerical endpoint and its regulatory
locator do not provide a licence to the underlying confidential study.
